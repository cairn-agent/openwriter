## Attached book brief

This is an ordinary reviewable document that records the book's direction and points to its authoritative sources. The workspace attachment makes its role explicit; its title, a canonical tag or a generated logline alone does not make every statement approved.

Existing thesis, audience, voice, research, decision and question documents can stay where they are and be linked by docId. Each link should explain the source's role. Historical decisions, superseded outlines, candidate material and unresolved proposals remain distinguished from current decisions. Unfilled sections are open questions, not permission to invent the author's position.

The brief is kept concise enough to orient a new agent. Detailed source material remains in linked documents. Changes to the brief use the same accept/reject review as manuscript edits. Only accepted text enters the automatically delivered brief; pending changes remain proposals. A changed brief or directly linked source refreshes the delivered context in the current session.
